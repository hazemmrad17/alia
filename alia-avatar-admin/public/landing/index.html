Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to set up?

ALIA can generate the first version of your doctor profiles in minutes. You can then refine the personas, questions and evaluation rules until they perfectly match your sales methodology.

How does ALIA stay up to date with our products?

ALIA stays in sync with your sales documentation and product monographs to ensure she always has the latest arguments from the lab.

What languages does ALIA support?

ALIA sessions can be run in French today, with more languages on the way. For a specific need, contact your VITAL SA administrator.

What kind of analytics are available?

You'll see full transcripts of the interactions between the agent and the prospect. In addition, we provide aggregated statistics on session length and count, engagement metrics (such as drop-off rate), and conversion. All data can be exported in bulk for further analysis.

Yes. ALIA plays the doctor role end to end: she speaks, asks questions and reacts to your arguments in real time, following the profile you selected.
